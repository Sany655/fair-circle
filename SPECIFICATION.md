# FairCircle (ফেয়ার-সার্কেল) — System Specification

## 1. Vision & Core Mission
To build an independent, self-regulating, parallel economic operating system that restores human dignity and justice by eliminating usury (*Riba*), compound interest debt, and speculative manipulation (*Gharar/Maysir*), anchoring all exchange in real, tangible physical assets.

---

## 2. Actors & Roles
1. **Citizen / Consumer:** Everyday human who buys ethical goods, shares tools, accesses 0% emergency loans, or pre-funds producers.
2. **Producer / Maker:** Farmers, artisans, weavers, independent clinics who supply real goods and services directly without middleman cartels.
3. **Decentralized Escrow Core:** An autonomous code-enforced multi-signature escrow holding funds during a 48-hour inspection period.
4. **Solidarity Reserve (Mutual Aid Pool):** Autonomous neighborhood reserve running on 0.00% APR with 100% repayment tracking based on trust and transparent communal records.

---

## 3. Product Architecture & Navigation
* **🛒 Marketplace:** Verified real-economy producers with transparent cost breakdowns (Raw Materials + Labor Hours + Fair Profit).
* **🌱 Invest & Pre-Order:** Forward production (*Salam*) enabling consumers to fund farms/workshops before harvest, cutting out bank loans.
* **🤝 Mutual Aid & Share:** 0% interest emergency loans (*Qard Hasan*) + local tool and machinery library.
* **📊 My Financial Health:** Unified single-screen calculator for wage-earners (paycheck shielding, debt defense, societal impact score, 2.5% wealth circulation duty).
* **ℹ️ How It Works:** Visual comparison of the Debt Pyramid ($1,000 created vs. $1,100 owed) vs. the Living Circle.

---

## 4. Currency Benchmark
* **Primary Currency:** Bangladeshi Taka (`৳ BDT`).
* **Underlying Stable Anchor:** Physical Gold ($\approx 85\text{ grams} \approx \text{৳}7,80,000$ Nisab circulation threshold).
