# FairCircle (ফেয়ার-সার্কেল)
### An Autonomous, Interest-Free, Real-Asset Circular Economy Platform

FairCircle is a transparent economic platform designed to free everyday people from predatory debt traps, compound interest (*Riba*), and cartel markups by returning to real, tangible value exchange.

---

## 🏛️ The Core Philosophy

### 1. The Debt Trap vs. The Living Circle
* **The Problem:** In the conventional banking system, money is printed as debt with interest ($1,000 issued, but $1,100 owed). Mathematically, the extra interest was never minted, legally forcing bankruptcies, home repossessions, and inflation onto ordinary citizens.
* **The Solution:** FairCircle is built on 100% tangible asset-backing, $0.00\%$ interest, and a mandatory $2.5\%$ wealth circulation rule (*Zakat/Anti-Hoarding*) on idle wealth over the gold threshold ($\approx \text{৳}7,80,000$).

---

## 📱 Platform Modules (Android + Web)

1. **🛒 Marketplace (মার্কেটপ্লেস):**
   * Direct purchases from farmers, artisans, and doctor clinics.
   * Transparent Price Breakdown: Raw Materials + Labor Hours + Fair Profit (Zero syndicate markup).
   * 48-Hour Inspection Escrow: Funds are held safely until the buyer receives and verifies the real item.

2. **🌱 Invest & Pre-Order (বিনিয়োগ ও প্রি-অর্ডার):**
   * Eliminates bank loans for producers (*Salam* forward-production model).
   * Everyday citizens pre-fund farm harvests or carpentry batches; in return, they receive fresh goods or shared profit at factory cost.

3. **🤝 Mutual Aid & Share (পারস্পরিক সহযোগিতা ও ধার):**
   * 0% Interest Benevolent Emergency Loans (*Qard Hasan* pool).
   * Community Tool & Machinery Library (sharing water pumps, drills, tillers for free or fuel cost).

4. **📊 My Financial Health (আমার আর্থিক স্বাস্থ্য ও প্রভাব):**
   * Single unified calculator for outside 9-to-5 wage earners.
   * Shows monthly paycheck shielding plan, debt defense to eliminate usury, and the $2.5\%$ community circulation balance.

5. **ℹ️ How It Works (About Animation):**
   * Interactive visual showing the transformation from the **Debt Pyramid** to the **Living Circle**.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture Plan

* **Frontend:** Android (Kotlin / Jetpack Compose) + Responsive Web (HTML5 / Tailwind / React)
* **Design Theme:** Pure White, high-contrast, accessible for any age or background.
* **Currency:** Native Bangladeshi Taka (`৳ BDT`).
* **Verification:** Decentralized 2-Sided Peer Escrow (no centralized gatekeepers).
